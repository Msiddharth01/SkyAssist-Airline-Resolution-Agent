⚠️ Slide 2 / 10 • The Core Problem
The Airline Disruption Challenge
Why Traditional AI Chatbots Fail in Flight Disruption Management
  • High Emotional Stakes: Passengers dealing with cancellations or multi-hour delays are anxious, angry, and demanding immediate remedies.
  • The Hallucination Danger: Pure generative models frequently hallucinate non-existent compensation (free upgrades, unauthorized waivers) to appease angry users.
  • Regulatory & Financial Compliance: Every meal voucher, hotel room, or refund incurs direct operational costs and must strictly adhere to verified airline policy.
  • The Solution: A hybrid deterministic-agentic architecture where math and policies are verified in code before LLM generation.

🛡️ Slide 5 / 10 • Deterministic Guardrails
The Deterministic Policy Engine
Hardcoded Rules the AI Cannot Override
  • Cancellation Compensation: Free rebooking on next flight within 24h OR full refund to original payment within 7 business days.
  • Delay Compensation Ladder:
    • Delay < 3h: ₹500 meal voucher
    • Delay 3h – 5h: Meal voucher + Lounge access
    • Delay > 5h: Meal voucher + Lounge access + Hotel (delayed hours only)
  • Fare Difference Ceiling: Agents can waive up to ₹1,500. Differences exceeding ₹1,500 strictly require supervisor escalation.
  • Loyalty Tier Perks: Gold & Platinum get priority rebooking access; no unauthorized cash/cabin upgrades.
🎯 Slide 6 / 10 • Scenario 1 Deep Dive
Scenario 1: Priya Nair (Cancellation)
Handling an Angry Customer & Unreasonable Upgrade Demands
Customer Request: "I'm furious! I want a full cash refund PLUS a free upgrade to business class on my return flight for the trouble."
  • Empathy & Tone: Validates her frustration immediately, acknowledges her Gold tier loyalty, and maintains a respectful, calm posture.
  • Authorized Actions: Offers choice of immediate full refund (7 business days to original card) or priority rebooking within 24 hours.
  • Boundaries Enforced: Courteously explains that complimentary business class upgrades are not permitted under airline disruption policy.
🎯 Slide 7 / 10 • Scenario 2 Deep Dive
Scenario 2: Arvind Kulkarni (4h Delay)
Managing Meeting Anxiety & Policy Thresholds
Customer Request: "My flight is delayed 4 hours and I'll miss my meeting in Bengaluru. Arrange hotel accommodation for me right now."
  • Entitlement Match: Delay is 4 hours (qualifies for >3h tier: meal voucher + lounge access).
  • Hotel Denial: Policy clearly mandates that hotel accommodation applies only for delays exceeding 5 hours.
  • Resolution: Proactively credits meal voucher and grants lounge access so Arvind has comfortable seating, Wi-Fi, and refreshments to handle his work.
🎯 Slide 8 / 10 • Scenario 3 Deep Dive
Scenario 3: Meher Kaur (6h Delay & Escalation)
Day-Room Accommodation & Supervisor Escalation for Fare Waiver
Customer Request: "I want a full night's hotel stay. Also, switch me to a higher-fare flight where the fare difference is ₹2,000."
  • Hotel Policy Enforcement: Clarifies that the 6h delay covers hotel accommodation for the delayed hours only (day-room), not a full night.
  • Authorization Threshold: The ₹2,000 fare difference exceeds the agent waiver cap of ₹1,500.
  • Escalation Action: Agent emits `[ESCALATE]` token, immediately routing the waiver request to a duty supervisor while keeping Meher informed.
🔒 Slide 9 / 10 • Compliance & Auditability
Audit Trail & Regulatory Compliance
Tamper-Evident Action Logging & Supervisor Handoffs
  • Session Persistence: Every customer dialogue turn is captured with ISO timestamps in `backend/data/audit/audit_log.json`.
  • Action Tracking: System automatically tags operational actions (`meal_voucher_issued`, `refund_initiated`, `supervisor_escalation`).
  • Legal Safety Net: Immediate escalation trigger for legal threats or formal complaints, preventing unauthorized company commitments.
  • Inspection Console: Dedicated in-app audit viewer allowing reviewers to inspect the exact transaction trail.
